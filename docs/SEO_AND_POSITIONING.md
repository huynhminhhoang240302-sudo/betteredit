# SEO and positioning

## Recommended identity

- Brand: **BetterEdit**
- Repository: `betteredit`
- Page title: **BetterEdit — Open-Source AI Video Editing Skill & 240 Motion Templates**
- Short description: **One installable, local-first video editing skill for Codex, with 240 playable MP4 templates, schemas, validation tools, and safety-first asset binding.**

## Why this positioning

The project is not another complete timeline editor. Its clearest category is a self-contained video-editing skill with an agent-friendly editing grammar and template library. The title leads with the common search phrase “AI video editing” and names the concrete differentiator: one skill with 240 motion templates.

## Repository topics

`ai-video-editing`, `video-editing`, `motion-graphics`, `coding-agents`, `codex`, `video-templates`, `local-first`, `json-schema`, `video-production`, `creative-coding`

Use the recommended title for clarity. Avoid branding the project “Codex Editor,” “OpenAI Video,” or similar names that could imply official affiliation.

> BetterEdit is an open-source, local-first video editing skill for Codex. One install provides 240 validated motion templates, playable previews, schemas, selectors, and safety rules.
