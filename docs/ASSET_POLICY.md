# Public asset policy

## Allowed

- deterministic procedural previews generated by BetterEdit scripts;
- original diagrams and screenshots created for the project;
- media under a license compatible with repository distribution, with attribution and license recorded;
- user contributions whose author confirms they have distribution rights.

## Not allowed

- reference videos used only for private analysis;
- downloaded stock footage without redistribution rights;
- client or unpublished media;
- copied interface assets, logos, fonts, music, or voice recordings without permission;
- datasets or transcripts containing personal information;
- assets whose provenance cannot be documented.

## Contribution record

Every non-procedural asset should have a provenance record containing source, author, license, modification summary, and attribution requirement.

## Generated media

Record the generator, model or tool, date, prompt category, and any relevant usage terms. Do not assume that “AI-generated” automatically means unrestricted.
