# Governance

BetterEdit begins with a maintainer-led governance model.

## Roles

- **Primary maintainer:** owns releases, security response, roadmap decisions, issue triage, and final merges.
- **Core maintainer:** has sustained responsibility for review, triage, releases, or a major subsystem.
- **Contributor:** submits issues, documentation, tests, templates, or code.

## Decision process

Routine changes use pull-request review. Material changes to schemas, permanent locks, licensing, privacy boundaries, or backward compatibility require a written design issue and a documented maintainer decision.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer status is earned through repeated, high-quality contributions and reliable review or triage work. It is not granted solely for one large contribution.

## Transparency

Releases, roadmap changes, security advisories, and breaking decisions should be public after any necessary coordinated disclosure period.
